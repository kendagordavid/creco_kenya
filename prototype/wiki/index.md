1. **Compile** — Approved PDFs are transformed into structured Markdown wiki pages.
2. **Search** — User questions are matched to relevant wiki pages using full-text search.
3. **Answer** — Responses are drawn from compiled wiki content with citations to both wiki pages and the original PDFs.
