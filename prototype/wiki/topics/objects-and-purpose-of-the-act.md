# Objects and Purpose of the Public Benefit Organizations Act

The **Public Benefit Organizations Act, 2013 (No. 18 of 2013)** is a key piece of legislation in Kenya that governs the registration, regulation, and oversight of organizations operating for public benefit. Understanding the objects and purpose of this Act is essential for anyone involved with or interested in public benefit organizations (PBOs).

## What are the Objects of the Act?

The objects of the Public Benefit Organizations Act are the fundamental goals that the legislation seeks to achieve. According to Section 3 of the Act:

- To provide a legal framework for the registration, regulation, and oversight of public benefit organizations in Kenya.
- To promote the development of a vibrant, transparent, and accountable public benefit sector.
- To ensure that public benefit organizations operate in a manner consistent with their stated purposes and the public interest.
- To facilitate the government's responsibility in supporting and regulating PBOs without undue interference.
- To encourage self-regulation and good governance within the public benefit sector.

These objects aim to balance the autonomy of PBOs with the need for accountability and transparency to the public and the government.

## Purpose of the Act

The purpose of the Act is to establish a clear and effective legal regime that:

- Defines what constitutes a public benefit organization.
- Sets out the requirements and procedures for registration of PBOs.
- Provides mechanisms for monitoring and ensuring compliance with the law.
- Establishes the Public Benefit Organizations Authority to oversee the sector.
- Encourages ethical conduct, fiscal transparency, and organizational integrity among PBOs.
- Protects the interests of the public and donors by ensuring that PBOs use resources for their intended public benefit purposes.

By doing so, the Act supports the growth of a trustworthy and efficient nonprofit sector that contributes positively to social, economic, and community development in Kenya.

## Key Points to Remember

- The Act applies specifically to organizations that operate for public benefit, as defined in the legislation.
- It promotes both government oversight and self-regulation by PBOs.
- The objects emphasize transparency, accountability, and good governance.
- The Act facilitates cooperation between the government and PBOs to enhance the sector's impact.
