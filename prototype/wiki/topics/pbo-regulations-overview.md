# Overview of the Public Benefit Organizations Regulations, 2026 for NGOs

The **Public Benefit Organizations Regulations, 2026** (Legal Notice No. 43) provide detailed rules and procedures for the registration, management, and oversight of Public Benefit Organizations (PBOs) in Kenya. These regulations are made under the authority of the **Public Benefit Organizations Act, 2013 (No. 18 of 2013)** and are essential for NGOs and other entities seeking to operate as PBOs.

---

## Purpose of the Regulations

The Regulations aim to:

- Establish a clear framework for managing and overseeing PBOs.
- Define criteria and procedures for registration, suspension, cancellation, and reinstatement of PBOs.
- Facilitate coordination of forums and federations of PBOs.

---

## Key Parts of the Regulations

### Part I: Preliminary

- **Citation and Interpretation**: Defines key terms such as "authorised agent," "asset," "forum," "federation," "permit," and "Register."
- **Object**: To provide a regulatory framework for PBOs' governance and coordination.

### Part II: Pre-requisites for Registration

- **Name Search and Reservation**: Applicants must apply for a name search and reservation with the Authority, proposing at least two names and paying the prescribed fee. Reserved names are valid for 60 days and may be extended.
- **Prohibited Names**: Names that are similar to existing PBOs, offensive, misleading, or imply connection to government entities are not allowed.
- **Public Benefit Test**: Activities must be genuinely for public benefit, aligned with the Act's Sixth Schedule, and must not provide private benefit to directors or associates.

### Part III: Registration of Public Benefit Organizations

- **Eligibility**: Groups must demonstrate intent to engage in public benefit activities and comply with registration requirements.
- **Application Process**: Applications must be submitted in the prescribed form with supporting documents including constitution, proof of name reservation, meeting minutes, director details (with at least one-third Kenyan nationals resident in Kenya), and addresses.
- **Authority's Role**: The Authority has 60 days to approve or reject applications and may impose conditions on registration certificates.

### Bestowment of PBO Status

- Entities already incorporated under other laws may apply for PBO status if they meet criteria such as good standing, at least three years of public benefit activity, and governance requirements.
- Applications require detailed documentation including constituting documents, registration certificates, audited accounts for the past three years, and director particulars.
- The Authority decides within 60 days and may issue a certificate of bestowment or reject the application.

---

## Other Important Provisions

- **International PBOs**: Separate eligibility and application procedures exist for international PBOs, including exemptions.
- **Obligations Upon Registration**: Registered PBOs must maintain proper records, submit annual reports, and notify the Authority of material changes.
- **Suspension, Cancellation, and Appeals**: The Regulations outline grounds and procedures for suspension or cancellation of registration, including rights of appeal.
- **Recognition of Forums and Federations**: Procedures for recognizing collective bodies of PBOs to enhance coordination.
- **Dissolution and Deregistration**: Rules governing voluntary dissolution, deregistration by the Authority or Court, and safeguarding of assets.
- **Miscellaneous**: Includes provisions on economic activities, electronic submissions, replacement of lost documents, penalties, and compliance by existing NGOs.

---

## How These Regulations Affect NGOs

NGOs intending to register or continue operating as PBOs must comply with these Regulations to:

- Secure and maintain valid registration.
- Ensure their activities meet the public benefit criteria.
- Keep transparent records and submit timely reports.
- Engage with recognized forums or federations where applicable.
- Understand the consequences of non-compliance, including penalties or deregistration.

For detailed procedural steps, forms, and fees, NGOs should refer to the **First and Second Schedules** of the Regulations.

## Muhtasari kwa Kiswahili

**Kanuni za Mashirika ya Umma za 2026** zinaweka miongozo ya usajili, usimamizi, na uendeshaji wa mashirika ya faida za umma (PBOs) nchini Kenya. Mashirika yasiyo ya kiserikali (NGOs) yanapaswa kufuata kanuni hizi ili kupata usajili halali, kuhakikisha shughuli zao ni za manufaa kwa umma, na kuwasilisha ripoti za kila mwaka. Kanuni pia zinaelezea taratibu za kusitisha, kufuta usajili, na kuanzisha mashirika ya pamoja (forums) kwa ajili ya ushirikiano bora. Kwa maelezo zaidi kuhusu michakato na ada, NGOs zinapaswa kurejea ratiba na fomu zilizotolewa katika Kanuni hizi.
