# Public Benefit Organizations Regulatory Authority and Public Registry

This page provides an overview of the **Public Benefit Organizations Regulatory Authority** and the **Public Registry** as established under the Public Benefit Organizations Act, 2013 (No. 18 of 2013) and supplemented by the Public Benefit Organizations Regulations, 2026. It explains the Authority’s establishment, functions, and the role of the public registry in the governance and oversight of Public Benefit Organizations (PBOs) in Kenya.

---

## Establishment of the Public Benefit Organizations Regulatory Authority

The Public Benefit Organizations Regulatory Authority (hereafter, "the Authority") is a statutory body established under **Section 34** of the Public Benefit Organizations Act, 2013.

- The Authority is responsible for the regulation, supervision, and facilitation of PBOs in Kenya.
- It operates under a Board appointed according to the provisions in **Sections 35 to 41** of the Act.
- The Board oversees the Authority’s functions and ensures compliance with the law.

### Composition and Governance

- The Board consists of members with qualifications and experience relevant to the PBO sector.
- Members serve fixed terms and take an oath of office to uphold their duties.
- The Board has powers to delegate functions and conduct its business as per the Act.

### Functions of the Authority

Outlined in **Section 42**, the Authority’s key functions include:

- Registering PBOs and maintaining an up-to-date public registry.
- Monitoring compliance with the Act and Regulations.
- Investigating complaints and taking enforcement actions.
- Promoting good governance, transparency, and accountability among PBOs.
- Facilitating capacity building and self-regulation initiatives within the sector.
- Collaborating with government agencies and stakeholders to support PBOs.

The Authority also has a Director and staff appointed to manage day-to-day operations (**Sections 45 and 46**).

---

## The Public Registry of Public Benefit Organizations

### Purpose and Maintenance

- The Authority maintains a **Register of Public Benefit Organizations** as mandated by **Section 15** of the Act.
- The Register is a public record that contains details of all registered PBOs, including their status, governance, and compliance information.
- It serves as an official source of information for the public, government agencies, and other stakeholders.

### Contents of the Register

The Register includes:

- The name and registration number of each PBO.
- The objects and purposes of the organization.
- Details of the governing body and key officials.
- Certificates of registration and any suspensions or cancellations.
- Records of annual reports and financial statements submitted.

### Access and Use

- The Register is accessible to the public to promote transparency.
- Official notices, letters, summons, or legal processes related to PBOs are served through the Authority using information from the Register (**Section 14**).
- The Register supports due diligence by donors, partners, and the public when engaging with PBOs.

---

## Legal Framework and Supporting Regulations

- The Authority’s operations and the management of the Register are further detailed in the **Public Benefit Organizations Regulations, 2026 (Legal Notice No. 43)**.
- These Regulations provide procedural guidelines for registration, reporting, compliance monitoring, and enforcement.

## Muhtasari kwa Kiswahili

**Mamlaka ya Usajili na Usimamizi wa Mashirika ya Umma (PBO Regulatory Authority)** ni taasisi iliyoundwa kwa mujibu wa Sheria ya Mashirika ya Umma ya 2013. Mamlaka hii inahusika na kusajili, kusimamia, na kufuatilia mashirika ya umma nchini Kenya. Inahakikisha mashirika haya yanafuata sheria, yana uwazi, na uwajibikaji katika shughuli zao.

**Sajili ya Mashirika ya Umma (Public Registry)** ni rekodi ya umma inayohifadhi taarifa zote muhimu kuhusu mashirika yaliyosajiliwa, ikiwa ni pamoja na taarifa za uongozi, hali ya usajili, na ripoti za kila mwaka. Sajili hii inapatikana kwa umma ili kuleta uwazi na kuimarisha imani kwa mashirika ya umma.

Kwa ujumla, mamlaka hii ni muhimu katika kuhakikisha mashirika ya umma yanatekeleza malengo yao kwa ufanisi na kwa kuzingatia sheria.
