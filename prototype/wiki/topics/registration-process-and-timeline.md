# Public Benefit Organization (PBO) Registration Process and Timeline

This page outlines the official process and timeline for registering a Public Benefit Organization (PBO) in Kenya under the **Public Benefit Organizations Act, 2013 (No. 18 of 2013)** and the **Public Benefit Organizations Regulations, 2026 (Legal Notice No. 43)**.

---

## Overview

A Public Benefit Organization (PBO) is a group of persons engaged in activities that provide a public benefit as defined by law. Registration is mandatory before operating as a PBO in Kenya. The registration process ensures that organizations meet legal requirements, including the nature of their activities, governance, and compliance with public benefit criteria.

---

## Step 1: Name Search and Reservation

Before applying for registration, the proposed name of the PBO must be searched and reserved with the PBO Authority.

- **Application:** Submit a written application to the Authority with at least two proposed names and pay the prescribed fee.
- **Authority Action:** The Authority conducts a name search.
  - If the name is available, it will be reserved for **60 days**.
  - If unavailable, the applicant is notified with reasons.
- **Extension:** The reservation can be extended for another 60 days upon written request and approval.

### Prohibited Names

The Authority will not register a PBO if the name:

- Is similar to an existing PBO.
- Constitutes an offence under any law.
- Is offensive or undesirable.
- Suggests connection to a government or public entity.

---

## Step 2: Public Benefit Test

The activities of the organization must satisfy the public benefit test, meaning:

- The activities fall within the public benefit categories defined in the Act.
- Beneficiaries are identifiable.
- Benefits are directly related to the public benefit purpose.
- No private benefit is conferred on directors, their families, or close associates.

If the organization has multiple purposes, each must independently meet the public benefit test.

---

## Step 3: Application for Registration

Once the name is reserved and the public benefit test is met, the organization may apply for registration.

### Requirements for Application

- **Form:** Submit the application using **Form 1** (Second Schedule).
- **Fee:** Pay the prescribed registration fee.
- **Documents to Attach:**
  - Constitution of the PBO (prepared per section 8(4) of the Act).
  - Proof of name reservation.
  - Minutes of the meeting resolving to register as a PBO.
  - Details of directors (at least one-third must be Kenyan nationals resident in Kenya).
  - Physical and postal addresses of the organization.

### Authority's Decision Timeline

- The Authority must determine the application within **60 days** of receipt.
- If approved, a **Certificate of Registration** (Form 2) is issued.
- If rejected, the applicant is notified in writing with reasons.
- The Authority may impose conditions on the registration certificate.

---

## Step 4: Bestowment of PBO Status (For Existing Entities)

Entities already incorporated under other laws may apply for bestowment of PBO status if they meet specific criteria:

- Incorporated and in good standing under Kenyan law.
- Not excluded under section 5(2) of the Act.
- Have operated public benefit activities for at least three years.
- Have a governing body with at least three persons, one-third being Kenyan nationals resident in Kenya.
- Activities fall within the Sixth Schedule categories of the Act.

### Application for Bestowment

- Use **Form 3** (Second Schedule).
- Pay the prescribed fee.
- Attach:
  - Constituting documents.
  - Certificate of incorporation.
  - Minutes resolving to seek PBO status.
  - Details of directors/officials.
  - Audited accounts for the last three years.
  - Physical and postal addresses.

The Authority will decide within **60 days**, issuing a **Certificate of Bestowment** (Form 4) if approved or notifying reasons for rejection.

---

## Summary of Timeline

| Step                          | Maximum Duration          |
|-------------------------------|--------------------------|
| Name search and reservation    | Reservation valid for 60 days (extension possible) |
| Registration application review| 60 days from application receipt |
| Bestowment application review  | 60 days from application receipt |

---

## Additional Notes

- The registration process is governed by the PBO Act, 2013 and the PBO Regulations, 2026.
- The Authority maintains a Register of Public Benefit Organizations.
- Registered PBOs must comply with ongoing obligations including record-keeping and reporting.

For more details, see [[PBO Act Overview]] and [[PBO Obligations]].
