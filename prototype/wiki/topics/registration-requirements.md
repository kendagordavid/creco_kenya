# Public Benefit Organization (PBO) Registration Requirements

This page outlines the legal requirements and procedures for registering a Public Benefit Organization (PBO) in Kenya under the **Public Benefit Organizations Act, 2013 (No. 18 of 2013)** and the **Public Benefit Organizations Regulations, 2026 (Legal Notice No. 43)**.

---

## What is a Public Benefit Organization?

A Public Benefit Organization (PBO) is an entity established for purposes that benefit the public, such as charitable, educational, religious, or community development activities. The Act defines the scope and nature of PBOs and sets the framework for their registration and regulation.

---

## Registration of Public Benefit Organizations

### Legal Basis for Registration

- The registration of PBOs is governed primarily by **Part II** of the Public Benefit Organizations Act, 2013.
- The registration process is administered by the **Public Benefit Organizations Authority** (the Authority), which is responsible for considering applications and granting registration certificates.

### Who Must Register?

- Any organization that intends to operate as a PBO in Kenya must apply for registration.
- This includes local organizations and international organizations operating within Kenya.

---

## Requirements for Registration

According to **Section 8 of the PBO Act (2013)** and supported by the **PBO Regulations (2026)**, an applicant must satisfy the following conditions:

1. **Application Form**  
   - Submit a completed application form prescribed by the Authority.

2. **Constitution or Governing Document**  
   - Provide a copy of the organization's constitution or governing document.
   - The constitution must clearly state the organization's objectives, which must be exclusively for public benefit purposes as defined by the Act.

3. **Details of Governing Body**  
   - Provide names, addresses, and other relevant details of the organization's governing body members.

4. **Physical Address**  
   - Provide the physical address of the organization’s main office in Kenya.

5. **Proof of Identity**  
   - Submit identification documents of the organization's key officials.

6. **Other Documents**  
   - Any other documents or information the Authority may require to verify the organization's eligibility.

---

## Application Consideration and Decision

- The Authority reviews the application to ensure compliance with the Act and Regulations.
- The Authority may request additional information or clarification during the review.
- If satisfied, the Authority grants a **Certificate of Registration** confirming the organization's status as a PBO.
- If the application is refused, the applicant has the right to seek a review or appeal as provided under the Act.

---

## Effect of Registration

- Once registered, the organization is recognized as a PBO and may enjoy benefits and privileges under the law.
- The organization must comply with ongoing reporting and regulatory requirements as stipulated in the Act and Regulations.
- Registration does not exempt the organization from other legal obligations but confirms its status for public benefit purposes.

---

## Registration of International Organizations

- International organizations operating in Kenya must also register as PBOs if they carry out public benefit activities.
- They must comply with the same registration requirements and provide additional documentation as required by the Authority.

---

## Maintaining Registration

- Registered PBOs must maintain compliance with the Act, including submitting annual reports and financial statements.
- The Authority has powers to suspend or cancel registration if an organization fails to comply with legal requirements.
