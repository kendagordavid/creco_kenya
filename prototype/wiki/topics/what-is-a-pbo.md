# What is a Public Benefit Organization (PBO)?

A **Public Benefit Organization (PBO)** is an organization established and operated primarily to promote the public good. The concept and legal definition of a PBO in Kenya are set out in the *Public Benefit Organizations Act, 2013* (No. 18 of 2013).

## Definition of a Public Benefit Organization

According to Section 5 of the *Public Benefit Organizations Act, 2013*, a Public Benefit Organization is an entity that:

- Is established and operated for purposes that are beneficial to the public.
- Does not operate for the profit or gain of its members.
- Has objectives that promote one or more public benefits as defined by the Act.

The Act emphasizes that PBOs must serve the public interest and contribute positively to society through activities such as:

- Relief of poverty
- Education
- Health care
- Environmental protection
- Human rights advocacy
- Community development
- Other charitable or social welfare activities

## Key Characteristics of a PBO

- **Non-profit nature:** PBOs do not distribute profits to members or directors. Any surplus must be reinvested in the organization’s public benefit activities.
- **Public benefit purpose:** The organization’s activities must be aimed at benefiting the public or a significant section of the public.
- **Registration:** PBOs must be registered under the Act to be recognized officially and to enjoy certain legal benefits and protections.
- **Accountability and transparency:** Registered PBOs are subject to reporting and regulatory requirements to ensure they operate ethically and transparently.

## Importance of PBOs

PBOs play a vital role in Kenya’s social and economic development by:

- Providing services and support where government resources may be limited.
- Advocating for marginalized and vulnerable groups.
- Promoting civic participation and community empowerment.
- Enhancing social cohesion and sustainable development.

## Muhtasari kwa Kiswahili

**Shirika la Manufaa ya Umma (PBO)** ni shirika lililoanzishwa na kuendeshwa kwa lengo la kuleta manufaa kwa umma kwa ujumla. Shirika hili halilengi kupata faida kwa wanachama wake, bali linalenga kusaidia jamii kupitia shughuli kama vile kupunguza umasikini, elimu, huduma za afya, na ulinzi wa mazingira. Sheria ya *Public Benefit Organizations Act, 2013* inaeleza masharti ya usajili na uendeshaji wa PBO ili kuhakikisha uwazi, uwajibikaji, na ufanisi katika kutoa huduma kwa umma.

Kwa hivyo, PBO ni taasisi muhimu inayochangia maendeleo ya kijamii na kiuchumi nchini Kenya.
